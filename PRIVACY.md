# Code[Mark] Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

Code[Mark] is a Markdown editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is designed to know
nothing about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Code[Mark] has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising,
no tracking and no crash-reporting service of its own. It does not send your
documents, their names, or anything about how you use the app to us or to anyone.

## Your documents

- Documents are opened and saved **where you keep them** — on your device, in iCloud
  Drive, or in another storage provider you choose in the Files app or Finder.
  Code[Mark] keeps no copy elsewhere.
- If you use iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider syncs the file under
  its own terms (for iCloud, Apple's privacy policy). Code[Mark] has no access to
  your account with it.
- Images you insert are stored **inside the Markdown file** itself.
- Diagrams and equations are rendered **on device**. Code[Mark] does not fetch
  remote images or other content referenced by a document.

## Permissions

- **Photos**: only when you choose *Insert › Image*, through the system photo picker,
  which shares only the images you select.
- **Folders**: when you allow access to a document's folder (so images stored next
  to it can be shown), that access stays on your device.

## Diagnostics

If you share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your device's
settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash data to us through App Store Connect
or TestFlight. You control this in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics &
Improvements*.

## Children

Code[Mark] collects no data from anyone, including children.
